America's OG Brewery

Thirty-plus years, eleven taprooms, one original idea.

The first Rock Bottom Restaurant & Brewery opened in the heart of Downtown Denver in 1991.

Fast-forward 30 years… Rock Bottom now has 11 locations across the United States, with thousands of original brews and hundreds of the industry's most esteemed medals and awards. We're a friendly neighborhood bar, a gourmet eatery, and an award-winning craft brewery — each location run by its own local, on-site Brewmaster tapping unique beers and seltzers in-house. Our chefs serve scratch-made dishes built to complement our award-winning pints, house-made cocktails, wine, and spirits.
